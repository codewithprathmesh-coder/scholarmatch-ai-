🎓 ScholarMatch AI

ScholarMatch AI is an AI-powered scholarship discovery and matching platform that helps students find scholarships that best match their profile.

🚀 What It Does

Students enter their details such as:

* Academic information
* Course and education level
* Family income
* Category
* Gender
* Location
* Other eligibility details

ScholarMatch AI analyzes the student’s profile and matches it with relevant scholarship opportunities.

✨ Key Features

* 🎯 Personalized Scholarship Matching — Find scholarships based on your profile.
* 🤖 AI-Powered Eligibility Analysis — Understand which scholarships are relevant to you.
* 📅 Deadline Tracking — Easily identify important scholarship deadlines.
* 📄 Required Documents — See documents needed for applications.
* ⚠️ Scholarship Conflict Detection — Identify potential conflicts between scholarships.
* 🔍 Eligibility Explanation — Understand why a scholarship matches your profile.
* 📊 Personalized Dashboard — View relevant scholarship opportunities in one place.

🎯 Problem

Students often miss scholarship opportunities because information is scattered across different websites, eligibility criteria can be confusing, and deadlines are easy to overlook.

ScholarMatch AI brings this information together and helps students discover opportunities that are relevant to them.

🌍 Our Vision

Every student should be able to discover the scholarships they are eligible for.
